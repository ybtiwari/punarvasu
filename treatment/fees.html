Fees & What's Included

Consultation fees are based on the nature and complexity of your case — confirmed once it has been reviewed, not before.

A fee that matches the case, not a fixed number.

Homeopathic treatment is not one-size-fits-all, and neither is its pricing. An acute complaint of a few days and a decade-long chronic condition involving multiple systems are not the same undertaking — they don't carry the same fee. Consultation fees typically range from ₹500 to ₹7,000, depending entirely on what your case actually requires.

The exact fee is confirmed once Dr. Tiwari has reviewed your case in full — not before, and not by guesswork.

What's included.

Every consultation fee covers the full course of care for that case, not just the first visit:

  • Thorough case-taking and direct consultation with Dr. Tiwari
  • Case review every 10–15 days as your treatment unfolds
  • Medicine adjustments as your case changes
  • A transparent prescription, with remedy names clearly given — never withheld
  • Follow-up support by phone or WhatsApp
  • No unnecessary lab investigations

Medicine, delivered — at no extra cost to you.

If it's more convenient, your medicine can be delivered directly to your home from the manufacturer. This is arranged purely as a convenience. Dr. Tiwari takes no commission or share of any kind — you pay only what the manufacturer charges, exactly as shown on your bill and on the medicine's own label.
