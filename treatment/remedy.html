Constitutional Remedy

One remedy — chosen to match the individual's constitution, not the disease name.

There is no fixed medicine for any disease.

There is no fixed medicine and no fixed dose in homeopathy. The dose must be matched to the strength of the disease. The medicine must be matched to the constitution of the patient. Two people with the same diagnosis will very likely receive different remedies — because their constitutions are different.

What "constitutional" means.

Your constitution is your particular way of being — your physical and mental characteristics, your sensitivities, your patterns of response to stress, illness, weather, and environment. The constitutional remedy is the one that most closely matches this total picture. When it is correct, it stimulates the body's own healing intelligence — not to suppress a symptom, but to begin reversing the disease process at its root.

A correct remedy does not fight the disease. It gives the organism what it needs to fight it itself — and win.

Homeopathic treatment is not a prescription.

It is a treatment — a process that unfolds over time. Unless the entire treatment is followed through its layers, lasting cure may not occur. It cannot be left unattended after a single prescription.

A single prescription is the beginning, not the whole.
