Treatment · Step 1

Case Taking

The first consultation is the foundation of everything that follows. It is a conversation — unhurried, thorough, and complete.

We do not begin with your diagnosis.

We begin with you. Your full history — the sequence in which conditions have appeared over your lifetime. What came first. What was suppressed. What followed. How you arrived at the state you are in now.

What we ask about.

The complete constitutional picture includes your physical symptoms — their location, sensation, what makes them better or worse, when they appeared. It also includes your mental and emotional state, your sleep, your appetite, your response to weather and environment, your life history, and the history of illnesses in your family.

Every detail is relevant. The remedy is chosen to match the whole person — so the whole person must be understood first.

How long it takes.

The first consultation typically takes 45 to 60 minutes. We do not rush this. A prescription made on an incomplete picture is a prescription made for the wrong person.

Come prepared to discuss your full history — not just what is troubling you now, but everything that has troubled you, for as long as you can remember.
