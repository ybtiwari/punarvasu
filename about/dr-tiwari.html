Qualifications and practice.

Dr. Yogeshwar Tiwari holds a Bachelor of Homeopathic Medicine and Surgery (BHMS) and has practised classical homeopathy in Ahmedabad for a number of years. His practice is rooted in constitutional prescribing — understanding the whole person before selecting any remedy.

The approach.

The practice does not follow fixed protocols or standard remedy lists for named conditions. Each case is taken in depth, and prescriptions are made based on what emerges from the individual's full constitutional picture — their physical symptoms, their mental and emotional state, their history, and their patterns of response.

The goal is not the management of symptoms. It is the complete reversal of the disease process — through natural principles, followed precisely and patiently.

The body is capable of more than it has been given credit for. My role is to understand its language — and give it what it needs to heal itself.

Who does well here.

Patients with chronic or recurring conditions who are willing to invest time in the process — attending follow-ups, observing their responses honestly, and communicating changes as they occur, including the return of old symptoms.
