Acute Conditions

Acute episodes are not separate from chronic disease. They share the same root — the individual's constitution.

Acute disease is a peak expression of the chronic state.

An acute episode — a recurring fever, a seasonal infection, a periodic digestive attack — is the expression of a constitutional tendency. The body is showing, repeatedly, where its susceptibility lies. These peaks are acute diseases, surfacing in a wave from the same chronic root.

What happens when acute episodes are suppressed.

If treated only symptomatically, the tendency behind the episode is not addressed. It remains. With each suppression and recurrence, it winds a little deeper. Episodes become more frequent. Eventually, what was occasional becomes permanent.

Treating an acute episode is the most efficient moment to address the constitutional tendency — while it is still close to the surface.

Why homeopathy is equally effective in acute conditions.

Treating an acute tendency early is considerably simpler than addressing it after years of deepening. The constitutional remedy addresses the tendency itself, at the point when it is easiest to reach.

Do not wait for an acute condition to become chronic. The earlier the intervention, the shallower the winding — and the more straightforward the cure.
