Conditions · Our Position

Why We Don't List Diseases

Because we do not treat diseases. We treat individuals.

The diagnosis is not the patient.

Two patients with the same diagnosis may receive completely different remedies here. The disease name tells us what the medical system has labelled their condition. It does not tell us who they are, how they got there, or what their constitution needs to reverse the process.

Listing diseases creates the wrong expectation.

When a clinic lists conditions, patients expect a medicine for their condition. That is not what we offer. We offer a process of constitutional treatment that addresses the underlying state producing those conditions — but the remedy will not be chosen because of the diagnosis.

The right question is not "what medicine do you use for my condition?" It is: "what do you need to know about me to find my remedy?"

What we do instead.

We describe the kinds of cases that come here — chronic, recurring, complex, multi-system, previously unresolved. If your condition fits that description and you are willing to follow the process, a consultation is the right next step.

If you are unsure whether your case is suitable, contact us. We will tell you honestly what to expect before you commit to a first consultation.
