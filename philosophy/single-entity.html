The Body as One Entity

Body and mind — with all their organs, systems, and parts — treated as a single, interdependent whole.

It may seem we are made of separate parts.

Different organs, different systems — each with its own specialist. But each part is interdependent and interconnected. They all work in close coordination. A dysfunction in one part is inevitably transferred to another — because they are not truly separate. They never were.

The body is one piece.

Think of the winding metaphor. A single piece of material being twisted. As it winds, the effect travels through the entire piece. There are no separate sections. The outermost layer and the innermost are the same material, connected throughout.

A dysfunctioning in one part gets inevitably transferred to another — the reason is that they are all interconnected and interdependent.

Homeopathy follows this principle.

We consider the body and mind — with all their organs, systems, and parts — as a single entity. When cure is to be affected, the entire totality of symptoms must be taken into consideration. The deeper state is cured first. The rest follows, in order.

There is one constitutional disturbance to address. The remedy is chosen for the whole — not for any one part of it.
