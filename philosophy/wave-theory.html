Wave Theory

The body is made of energy. Energy travels in waves. Disease and cure follow this same natural law.

Why does our body behave in a wave pattern?

Because our body is made of energy. And energy, by its very nature, travels in waves — not in a straight line. Every process in the body — heartbeat, breath, nerve impulse — is a wave. Disease and cure are no different. Homeopathy is an energy medicine and a naturally healing therapy. It follows natural laws.

How disease follows the wave.

When a disease first appears, its acute peak episodes occur less frequently. As the disease deepens over time, those episodes come closer together — more frequent, more intense.

These peaks are acute diseases. Acute disease is not a separate event — it is a peak expression of the chronic state of health. They are the same disturbance, surfacing in a wave as peaks.

How cure follows the wave.

When the disease is being cured through homeopathy, the episodes reduce in frequency and intensity. They appear at longer intervals, with progressively lesser force — until they stop appearing altogether. As cure completes, the morbid energy is removed and healthy energy is restored. Once the morbid energy stops oscillating, the body returns to its natural wavelength — without discomfort.
