How Cure Happens

Cure is not the removal of symptoms. It is the reversal of the process that produced them.

Disease developed through a process.

Removing it also requires a process. It can be undone faster than it developed — but it still has to follow a process. Every natural cure follows natural law. We are not blocking. We are curing — and curing has a process.

Cure travels in reverse.

From more important organs to less important organs. Inside out. In the reverse order of appearance of symptoms. Old symptoms may briefly return during this process — this is not a worsening, it is the disease being pushed outward. The reversal may be so swift it goes unnoticed. But it does reverse, always moving toward the less important organs.

When a patient says "my old skin condition came back" during treatment — that is often the best news of the consultation. It means the internal disease is moving outward.

Suppressing is easy. Curing is hard.

In homeopathy, a symptom will not disappear unless the disease behind it has resolved to that extent. If the disease is not cured, the symptoms will not go. We preserve symptoms unmasked — they are the measuring parameter of the curative process.

The goal is not comfort during illness. The goal is freedom from illness. These are not the same thing.
